# Product

## Register

product

## Users

CaresLink AI serves authenticated Australian care and support providers, plus referral operations and platform administrators. Providers use the product while completing focused documentation or referral tasks; administrators manage access and review aggregate operational metadata without reading provider-authored document content.

## Product Purpose

CaresLink AI is a focused workspace for turning de-identified support facts and provider-supplied information into review-ready document drafts and referral materials. It reduces blank-page work while keeping the human user responsible for checking, correcting and moving any completed record into the organisation's authorised system.

Success means a user can finish one bounded task with a clear privacy review, understand exactly what the product did and did not do, and retain control over whether any draft is copied, saved or used.

## Brand Personality

Calm, trustworthy and practical. The voice should feel like a careful operational colleague: direct about boundaries, respectful of care-sector context and never theatrical about AI.

## Anti-references

- Not a generic chatbot or an open-ended prompt playground.
- Not a clinical decision, risk assessment, care planning, legal or compliance tool.
- Not a system that implies a draft is approved, complete or automatically suitable for an official record.
- Not a flashy consumer-AI interface with decorative automation, opaque processing or exaggerated claims.
- Not an administrator surveillance surface for reading provider-authored content.

## Design Principles

1. **One task at a time.** Each screen should help the user complete one clearly named operational job.
2. **Privacy before processing.** Make de-identification, obvious-identifier findings and user confirmations visible before any network generation can become available.
3. **Human review is part of the workflow.** Inputs, cleaned facts and drafts must remain editable and visibly subject to user judgment.
4. **Boundaries are product features.** State unavailable capabilities and trust limits plainly; never simulate an AI result or silently relax a safety gate.
5. **Earn trust through consistency.** Reuse familiar controls, restrained visual hierarchy and the existing workspace vocabulary rather than inventing novel interaction patterns.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The product supports keyboard operation, visible focus states, responsive layouts and reduced-motion preferences. Status and validation feedback must not rely on colour alone and should be announced with appropriate semantics. English, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese experiences must use explicit locale copy; unsupported or missing translations must not silently fall back within a three-locale workflow. WCAG conformance has not yet been formally certified, so new work should be tested against WCAG 2.2 AA expectations without claiming certification.
